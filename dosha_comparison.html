Engine Feature Analysis

Vedic Astrology Doshas

Comparison of planetary afflictions and compatibility overrides calculated by Jyotish Flutter Library (SV-stark Fork) and PyJHora (naturalstupid)
Jyotish Flutter Library

A native, high-performance Dart engine designed for mobile (iOS/Android), web, and desktop. Focuses heavily on production-readiness for mobile apps, offering high-precision astronomical foundations and marriage compatibility (Ashtakoota Milan) including traditional doshas and complex overrides.

  • Standard Manglik (Kuja) Dosha checks with 5 cancellations.
  • Traditional Nadi Dosha + extensive Nakshatra/Pada cancels.
  • Traditional Bhakoot (Rashi) Dosha + Lord/Friendship cancels.
  • Direct integration into 36-point Guna Milan matching system.
PyJHora (Python)

An open-source Python library and desktop/CLI package inspired by PVR Narasimha Rao's book Vedic Astrology - An Integrated Approach and Jagannatha Hora software. Focuses heavily on single-chart analysis, astrological research, and implementing a vast set of classical text combinations.

  • Standalone dosha.py module with 8 distinct dosha checks.
  • Complete Kala Sarpa Dosha axis (12 types) calculations.
  • Extensive Manglik check with 17 BV Raman exemption rules.
  • Advanced single-chart doshas (Pitru, Guru Chandala, Shrapit, etc.).

Key Functional Separation

Your library is optimized for **marriage matchmaking (Ashtakoota Milan)**, implementing the crucial compatibility-based overrides (Nadi & Bhakoot Dosha) that modify the 36 Guna compatibility score. In contrast, PyJHora focuses primarily on **individual chart afflictions (Lagna/Natal Doshas)**, looking at combinations that impact a single person's horoscope (e.g. ancestral karma via Pitru Dosha, planetary alignments via Kala Sarpa, or specific bad conjunctions like Ghata and Shrapit).

Deep Dive: Calculation Rules compared
Manglik Dosha (Kuja Dosha / Mangal Dosh)
Jyotish PyJHora

This is checked in both libraries but with a different set of cancellation rules and options. Jyotish uses 5 standard rules based on BPHS/Phaladeepika, whereas PyJHora implements 17 detailed BV Raman exceptions.

Jyotish Flutter Implementation

Calculates Mars placement in houses 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, or 12 from Ascendant, Moon, and Venus. The severity is 'High' if present in 2 or 3 charts, and 'Moderate' if present in 1 chart.

Cancellations (Pariharas) Check:
  • Mars in Aries, Scorpio (own signs) or Capricorn (exalted).
  • Mars conjunct (same sign as) Jupiter or Moon.
  • Mars in Leo or Aquarius (cancels dosha in houses 7 and 8).
  • Mars aspected by Jupiter or Venus (checks 7th sign whole aspect).
  • Mars is the Lagna Lord (Aries or Scorpio Ascendant).
  • Matchmaking: Cancellations are aggregated at compatibility level (e.g., both partners Manglik). Also, Nadi Dosha override cancels Manglik.
PyJHora Implementation

Calculates Mars placement in houses 2, 4, 7, 8, 12 from a reference planet. Includes option to add 1st house (lagna house is omitted by default per Sanjay Rath's commentary). Allows choosing Venus, Moon, or Lagna as reference.

17 BV Raman Exemptions:
  • Mars in Leo or Aquarius (Exemption 1).
  • Mars in 2nd in Gemini/Virgo, 4th in Aries/Scorpio, 7th in Cancer/Capricorn, 8th in Sagittarius/Pisces, 12th in Taurus/Libra (Exemptions 2-6).
  • Mars conjoined or aspected by Jupiter or Saturn.
  • Retrograde Mars or weak Mars (combust, Rasi Sandhi).
  • Mars is Lagna Lord.
  • Mars in own, exalted, or friendly sign.
  • Mars in movable sign.
  • Dispositor rules (currently hardcoded as False in python script).
  • Jupiter or Venus in Lagna, or Mars conjoined with Jupiter/Moon.
Kala Sarpa Dosha (Kalsarp Dosh)
PyJHora Only

A classical astrological flaw formed when all planets are sandwiched between Rahu and Ketu, making them passive and restricting their positive effects.

Jyotish Flutter

Not natively implemented in the analysis packages. While longitudes of Rahu, Ketu, and all planets are calculated at the core engine level, no specialized service method exits to flag or identify this condition.

PyJHora Implementation

Fully implemented via the kala_sarpa(house_to_planet_list) helper function. It checks if the houses of all planets from Sun to Saturn lie sequentially between Rahu's house and Ketu's house.

Calculates 12 Sub-Types (by Rahu position):
  • 1st House: Anant Kala Sarpa
  • 2nd House: Kulik Kala Sarpa
  • 3rd & 4th Houses: Vasuki & Shankhapal
  • 5th & 6th Houses: Padma & Mahapadma
  • 7th & 8th Houses: Takshak & Karkotak
  • 9th to 12th Houses: Shankhachur, Ghatak, Vishdhar, & Sheshnag
Nadi Dosha (Vedic Compatibility Flaw)
Jyotish (Primary) PyJHora (Implicit)

Crucial for marital compatibility. If both partners have the same Nadi (Vata/Adi, Pitta/Madhya, Kapha/Antya), it causes severe energy and health clashes, reducing matchmaking scores by 8 points (out of 36).

Jyotish Flutter Implementation

Calculates the Nadi classification from Nakshatra index modulo 3: Adi (index % 3 = 0), Madhya (index % 3 = 1), Antya (index % 3 = 2). Implements native cancellations:

Cancellations Checked:
  • Rule 1: Same Nakshatra, but different Padas.
  • Rule 2: Same Moon sign (Rashi), but different Nakshatras.

If cancelled, Nadi compatibility score is restored from 0 to 8 points.

PyJHora Implementation

Nadi Dosha is not a standalone function in dosha.py. Instead, it is evaluated inside the star-compatibility module (match_ui.py) using Tamil Porutham (or Koota Milan) mapping. Cancellation rules are implicit in the Nakshatra lookup table but are not calculated dynamically using detailed chart longitude conditions like Pada offsets.

Bhakoot Dosha (Rashi / Moon Sign Relationship Flaw)
Jyotish (Primary) PyJHora (Implicit)

Checked during compatibility. If Moon signs are in 2/12, 5/9, or 6/8 relative distances, it causes financial, emotional, or health friction, subtracting 7 points from compatibility.

Jyotish Flutter Implementation

Specifically checks forward and reverse Moon sign distances between boy and girl. If 2/12, 5/9, or 6/8 relationships are found, it triggers Bhakoot Dosha. Implements full cancellation rules:

Cancellations Checked:
  • Same Sign Lord (e.g. Taurus & Libra ruled by Venus, Scorpio & Aries ruled by Mars).
  • Sign Lords are mutual friends (e.g., Sun & Moon, Jupiter & Mars).

If cancelled, compatibility score restores from 0 to 7 points.

PyJHora Implementation

Bhakoot check is not exposed in `dosha.py` but is handled within the match_ui.py compatibility UI. It checks basic star matches (Kootas) but does not expose detailed Lord friendship-based overrides directly as programmatic variables.

PyJHora Exclusive Individual Doshas (Pitru, Guru Chandala, Ganda Moola, etc.)
PyJHora Only

PyJHora has dedicated algorithms for identifying single-chart natal afflictions that describe personal spiritual barriers or karmic lessons, which are not currently implemented in the Jyotish Flutter library:

1. Pitru Dosha (Ancestral karma)

Indicates negative ancestral influences due to unresolved karmas. Triggered if:

  • Sun, Moon, or Rahu is in 9th house (house of father/dharma).
  • Ketu is in 4th house (house of mother/roots).
  • Sun, Moon, Rahu, or Ketu is conjoined/aspected by Mars or Saturn.
  • Venus, Mercury, or Rahu (any two) reside in 2nd, 5th, 9th, or 12th.
2. Guru Chandala Dosha

Indicates disruption in guru guidance and wisdom. Formed when Rahu or Ketu conjoins Jupiter in the same house. Calculates whether Jupiter's strength dominates the node to mitigate the flaw.

3. Ganda Moola Dosha

Triggered if born under Moon Nakshatras ruled by Ketu (Ashwini, Magha, Moola) or Mercury (Ashlesha, Jyeshtha, Revati). Signifies planetary transition zones (sandhis) which can cause early life challenges.

4. Kalathra Dosha (Marital affliction in single chart)

Formed when malefics (Sun, Mars, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu) occupy the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th, or 12th houses from Ascendant or Moon.

5. Ghata & Shrapit Doshas (Bad conjunctions)
  • Ghata Dosha: Mars and Saturn conjoined in the same house.
  • Shrapit Dosha: Saturn and Rahu conjoined in the same house (cursed combination).
Developer Summary & Guidance

Depending on your application's target use-cases, you can choose how to leverage these differences:

1. For Marriage Matching Apps

Use the Jyotish Flutter Library. It calculates the necessary Nadi and Bhakoot overrides and includes precise planetary longitudinal checks for Manglik cancellation. These are essential to output correct Guna Milan scores out of 36.

2. For Natal Horoscope Analysis

If you want to support report generation for single horoscopes (e.g., "Kalsarp Dosh" or "Guru Chandal Dosh" warnings), you can port the Python functions in PyJHora's dosha.py to your Dart library. Since your core engine already outputs exact planet coordinates and house charts, writing the Dart equivalent of these dosha rules is trivial.
