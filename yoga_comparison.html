Summary of Yoga Implementations

There is a stark contrast in how both libraries approach **Yoga detection**:

• **PyJHora (Python)** provides an **incredibly extensive natal yoga scanner** (detecting 284+ standard combinations sourced from B.V. Raman's Three Hundred Important Combinations). It scans divisional charts for yogas like Gajakesari, Sunapha, and Nabhasa yogas. However, its progeny-specific yogas are not isolated into a standalone model.

• **Jyotish (Flutter Fork)** does **not** feature a generic natal yoga scanner. Instead, it offers **specialized target yogas** in distinct domain modules: 6 electional **Muhurta Yogas**, 16 annual **Tajaka Yogas** (such as Itthasala and Nakta), 4 progeny-specific **Child Yogas**, and 27 daily astronomical **Panchanga Yogas**. If you need to detect standard life yogas (e.g. Raja Yoga, Pancha Mahapurusha), you will currently need to port these rules from PyJHora's open-source logic.
